# Support Ticket Escalation Policy

## Priority Levels
- **Low**: Cosmetic issues or general questions. First response within 48 hours.
- **Medium**: Functional issues that do not block product use. First response within
  24 hours.
- **High**: Product is unusable, safety concern, or the customer explicitly requests a
  refund. First response within 4 hours.

## When to Escalate
A ticket must be escalated to a Tier 2 specialist when any of the following are true:
- The ticket has been open for more than 5 business days without resolution.
- The customer has contacted support 3 or more times about the same order.
- The issue involves a safety hazard (e.g., electrical fault, structural failure).
- The requested refund amount exceeds $250.
- The customer explicitly asks to speak with a manager.

## Escalation Path
1. Tier 1 agent tags the ticket as `escalated` and assigns it to the Tier 2 queue.
2. Tier 2 specialist must acknowledge the escalation within 2 business hours.
3. If unresolved after 3 additional business days, the ticket is escalated to the
   Customer Experience Manager.
4. High-priority safety-related escalations bypass Tier 2 and go directly to the
   Customer Experience Manager.

## Communication Requirements
Customers must receive a status update at least once every 2 business days while a
ticket remains open, regardless of priority level. Escalated tickets require a status
update every business day.

## Closure Criteria
A ticket may be closed only when the customer confirms resolution, or after 3 attempts
to reach the customer over 10 business days with no response ("customer unresponsive"
closure).
