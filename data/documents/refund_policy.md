# Refund Policy

## Eligibility Window
Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of the delivery date shown on
their order. Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase are not eligible for
refund, regardless of condition.

## Condition Requirements
Products must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories included.
Items showing damage caused by misuse are eligible only for a partial refund of 50% of
the purchase price. Items that are defective on arrival (DOA) qualify for a full refund
or a free replacement, at the customer's choice.

## Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the
returned item passes warehouse inspection. Store credit refunds, when requested instead
of the original payment method, are processed within 1-2 business days and include a 5%
bonus credit.

## Non-Refundable Categories
- Gift cards and promotional credits
- Digital downloads and software licenses once activated
- Furniture items after 45 days from delivery, per the extended furniture allowance

## Furniture Extended Window
Furniture products (e.g., desks, chairs) have an extended refund window of 45 days
instead of the standard 30 days, because assembly and use often take longer to reveal
defects.

## Restocking Fee
A 10% restocking fee applies to electronics returned for reasons other than defect or
DOA (e.g., customer changed their mind). No restocking fee applies to furniture returns.

## Process
1. Customer opens a support ticket referencing the order ID.
2. Support agent verifies eligibility against this policy.
3. Customer ships the item back using the provided prepaid label.
4. Warehouse inspects the item and confirms condition.
5. Refund or replacement is issued per the rules above.
