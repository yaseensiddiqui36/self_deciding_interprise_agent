# Customer Support Guide

## Handling Order Status Questions
Check the `orders` table for the order's current status. Statuses used are: `shipped`,
`delivered`, `cancelled`, and `returned`. If a customer says an order shows "delivered"
but they have not received it, open a high-priority investigation ticket rather than a
standard inquiry.

## Handling Defective Product Reports
1. Confirm the order and product from the customer's account.
2. Ask for a photo of the defect when possible.
3. Apply the DOA rule from the refund policy if the defect was present on arrival.
4. If the customer reports a defect more than 45 days after delivery, offer a repair or
   discounted replacement instead of a refund, since it falls outside the refund
   eligibility window for most categories.

## Tone and Communication Guidelines
- Always acknowledge the customer's frustration before offering a solution.
- Avoid promising specific refund timelines beyond what the refund policy states.
- Never disclose another customer's order or account information.

## Common Issue Playbook
- **Wrong item received**: Offer free return shipping and expedited replacement.
- **Missing item from multi-item order**: Verify against the order quantity, then ship
  the missing unit at no cost without requiring a return of the rest of the order.
- **Noisy or malfunctioning furniture**: Treat as a defect claim; furniture defect
  claims use the 45-day extended window described in the refund policy.
- **Billing discrepancy**: Route to the billing team; do not attempt to resolve
  pricing disputes directly in a support ticket.

## Working with the Escalation Policy
Support agents should consult the escalation policy whenever a ticket priority is
"high" or a customer has opened multiple tickets for the same order. When in doubt
about whether to escalate, err on the side of escalating rather than leaving the
customer unresolved.
